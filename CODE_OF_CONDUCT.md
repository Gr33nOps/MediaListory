# Code of Conduct

MediaListory is a small project maintained by one person. This document is short because the expectations are simple.

## What is expected

Be straightforward and be civil. Assume the person on the other end is acting in good faith and has less context than you do. Criticise the code, the design, or the decision, never the person who wrote it. If you disagree, say what you would do instead and why.

Remember that most people here are doing this in their spare time. A pull request that sits for a while has not been ignored on purpose.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind. Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments. Sexual language or imagery. Discrimination or slurs relating to age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation. Publishing anyone's private information without their permission. Sustained disruption of issues or discussions.

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and anywhere else the project is being discussed.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by emailing the maintainer through the address on the [Gr33nOps GitHub profile](https://github.com/Gr33nOps), or by opening a private security advisory if you would rather it not be visible. Reports are read by the maintainer and are not shared further.

Depending on what happened, the response may be a warning, having a comment removed, or being blocked from the repository. Serious or repeated behaviour goes straight to a block.
