How it works

Browse or search any of the four libraries — no account needed to look around. When you find something, add it to your list with a status (watching, completed, plan to watch, on hold, or dropped), give it a score out of 10, jot notes, and track episodes for series and anime. Your profile shows a breakdown by media type, and you can follow other people to see what they are enjoying.

Frequently asked questions

Is MediaListory free?

Yes. Browsing, tracking, rating, lists, and profiles are all free.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to browse and view details. You only need a free account to save items, rate them, and build your library.

Where does the data come from?

Movies and series come from TMDB, anime from Kitsu, and games from IGDB. All artwork and metadata belong to their respective owners.

Can I track anime episode progress?

Yes. Series and anime support per-item episode progress, so you can record exactly where you are.

How do I sign in with Google or GitHub?

On the sign-in page, choose “Continue with Google” or “Continue with GitHub.” It’s a secure, standard OAuth flow — we never see your provider password.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Your library can be exported as JSON from your profile.
